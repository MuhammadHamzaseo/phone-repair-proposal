📝 Final Verdict

The developer did the basics (WordPress setup, SSL, sitemap, analytics, 404 handling) but left critical SEO and performance work incomplete.

The site is not ready for competitive ranking in the UAE perfume market because:

  1. Meta descriptions are completely missing — Google will auto-generate poor snippets, killing click-through rates.
  2. Product structured data is absent — Google won't show prices, ratings, or stock status in search results.
  3. Images are unoptimized — A single 1.7 MB PNG and zero lazy loading will destroy mobile PageSpeed scores.
  4. Security headers are missing — The site is vulnerable to basic attacks.
Bottom line: It looks like a standard WordPress/WooCommerce installation with minimal optimization. The client should request the developer or an SEO specialist to resolve the P0 and P1 issues immediately.
